01 — Voicemail craft

The rules that make them call back

01

Under 30 seconds. Speed-read: 150 words/min → ~60 words max. Every extra word buys more delete time.

02

Open with their name. "Hi {Name}" — it's the difference between "recorded sales pitch" and "a person called."

03

One curiosity hook. A peer-pain, a named outcome, or a number. Never a feature list.

04

One CTA. Call {Phone}. Not "call or email or check the link or…" One door.

05

Say it once. A voicemail isn't a call transcript. Leave texture they can't skim-read.

06

Always follow the cadence. Day 0 first contact → Day 2 value replay → Day 5–7 a third scenario, then rotate across channels.

02 — Bonus

If they pick up — 10-second cold-call openers

Voicemail writing trains the cold-call muscle. Here's the live-pickup version of the same thinking.

C-Level

"{Name}, {Your} at NetCom — one line: workforce readiness is the real bottleneck behind {their initiative}. 15 minutes, one page, a 90-day ROI model. Tuesday or Thursday?"

VP

"{Name}, {Your} — quick one: headcount's frozen but the targets aren't. We upskill the team you have with authorized Microsoft/AWS/Cisco training. 15 minutes this week?"

Director

"{Name}, {Your} — most Directors run programs with sub-40% completion. Ours map to certs and show live dashboards. 15 minutes to compare notes?"

Manager

"{Name}, {Your} — your team can get certified without losing the work week: ILT, virtual or self-paced. Show you how managers in {industry} do it. 15 minutes?"
