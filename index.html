01 — Who we are

The enterprise training arm of the Sarder Ecosystem

NetCom Learning is Sarder Inc DBA NetCom Learning, HQ'd at 1460 Broadway, New York. Our purpose: promote lifelong learning for every person, everywhere — mission: educate and certify 1 billion learners by 2035. We deliver authorized, instructor-led, role-based training aligned to real jobs, translated into measurable business outcomes.

Vendor-authorized training

Official curriculum from Microsoft, AWS, Cisco, Google Cloud, CompTIA, PMI, EC-Council, ISC2, AI CERTs™ and 60+ more — taught by certified instructors.

AI readiness & adoption

AI CERTs™ certifications (AI+, Bitcoin+), the N+ AI-native eLearning platform, Adoptify AI consulting, Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption journeys.

Enterprise scale

Fortune 1000 clients, multi-country delivery, managed learning services, LMS (NetCom365), funding programs (Learning Passport, EdAssist, COOL).
