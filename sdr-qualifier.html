01 — Read the room

How each level buys — speak their language

Same product, six different conversations. The C-Level wants outcomes; the Manager wants schedules; the practitioner wants the cert; procurement wants the evidence. Adapt the emphasis, never the honesty.

02 — Qualification framework

BANT — but asked like a consultant, not a checklist

B

Budget

Is there money, who owns it, what line does it sit under, does funding (ESI/ECIF, COOL, EdAssist) change the math?

A

Authority

Can this person approve, or are they a champion who sells it upward? Get the approver's name in the room.

N

Need

What is the skill gap costing them — cert deadlines, stalled AI, compliance, attrition? Problem before product.

T

Timeline

Quarters, audits, launches, cert-expiry cycles. Urgency is what turns interest into a calendar invite.

Pre-call qualification (before you even dial): check firmographics (revenue, size, industry), their tech stack signals (Azure vs AWS vs GCP vs on-prem — read their job posts & vendor pages), recent hires (did they just hire an AI/cloud lead?), certifications they advertise, and news/intent (funding rounds, audits, new HQ). The opener lands harder when the first line references something specific about them.
