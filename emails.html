01 — Before you send

Email etiquette that protects your reply rate

These templates do the heavy lifting, but sender behavior decides deliverability. Keep the list short and honest.

01

Speed over volume

Send inside 24h of the touch that triggers the email — the voicemail is still warm. A perfect email tomorrow loses to a good one today.

02

One ask per email

From addresses that sound like a human, a signature with a real phone number. "Reply and I'll take it from there" beats "visit our site."

03

Send like a peer

Tuesday–Thursday, early morning or early afternoon, local time. Avoid Monday 9am inbox floods and Friday pre-weekend graveyards.

02 — Subject lines that survive the inbox

Write like you're texting — not announcing

≤ 8 words

Mobile and preview panes truncate around 30–50 characters. Lead with their interest, not your company name.

One concrete thing

A number, a deadline, their news. "On {Company}'s AI rollout" beats "Training solutions intro."

No spam words

Free, guarantee, 50% off, urgent — they poison deliverability and get skimmed as noise.

Lowercase energy

Sentence case reads human. ALL CAPS reads invoice; "FW: FW: RE:" reads someone who never leaves.
